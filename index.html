About Cuore del Sud

Based in Battipaglia, we blend tradition with modern export expertise. Our team works closely with local farms and artisans to deliver consistent supply, transparent traceability, and a genuine taste of Southern Italy.

Local network

Longstanding partnerships with dairies and agricultural cooperatives guarantee seasonal excellence and reliable volumes.

Export expertise

Multilingual export managers guide documentation, customs, and compliance from quotation to delivery.

Sustainability

We prioritize short supply chains, recyclable packaging, and reduced food miles for a healthier planet.
